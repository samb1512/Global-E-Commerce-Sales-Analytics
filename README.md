# Global-E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics

An interactive **Tableau dashboard** developed to analyze global e-commerce sales performance, regional trends, product performance, customer behavior, and key business metrics.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify important patterns in sales performance across different regions, product categories, and customer segments.

The Tableau dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations and KPIs that help users understand business performance and identify areas of growth and improvement.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
* Identify high-performing regions and markets
* Analyze sales trends over time
* Compare product and category performance
* Understand customer purchasing patterns
* Identify key sales and revenue drivers
* Present business insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard

## Tools & Technologies

* **Tableau** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data source and data preparation
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts and analytical reporting
* **Exploratory Data Analysis** – Identifying sales and customer patterns

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a visual overview of global e-commerce business performance.

### Key Performance Indicators

The analysis focuses on important business metrics such as:

* Total Sales
* Revenue Performance
* Sales Trends
* Regional Performance
* Product Performance
* Customer-related metrics

### Dashboard Analysis

The Tableau workbook includes analysis of:

* Regional Sales Performance
* Sales Trends
* Product Performance
* Category-wise Sales
* Customer Analysis
* Revenue and Sales Distribution
* Comparative Business Performance

## Key Analytical Questions

This dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

1. Which regions generate the highest sales?
2. How do sales change over time?
3. Which product categories perform the best?
4. Which products contribute most to overall sales?
5. Which regions or markets have the strongest performance?
6. What patterns can be observed in customer purchasing behavior?
7. Which areas represent potential opportunities for business growth?

## Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

* High-performing and low-performing regions
* Sales trends and changes over time
* Top-performing product categories
* Products contributing significantly to overall sales
* Differences in regional and product performance
* Business areas that may require further investigation

> Specific numerical insights should be interpreted based on the dataset and the filters applied within the Tableau dashboard.
